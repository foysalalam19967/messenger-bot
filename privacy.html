Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Israt's World" Messenger bot ("we", "our", "the App") collects, uses, and protects information when you interact with our Facebook Page through Messenger.

1. Information We Collect

When you send a message to our Facebook Page, we may receive and store:

  • Your Facebook-assigned sender ID (a unique identifier, not your personal profile information)
  • The text content of the messages you send to us
  • Timestamps of when messages are sent and received

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal information beyond what is necessary to respond to your message.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

  • Automatically respond to your messages based on keywords
  • Improve the quality and relevance of our automated responses

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage and Security

Messages are processed in real-time to generate automated replies. We take reasonable technical measures to protect any information that passes through our system.

4. Third-Party Services

This bot uses Meta's Messenger Platform API to send and receive messages. Your use of Messenger is also subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

5. Your Rights

You may stop interacting with our Page at any time by simply not sending further messages. If you would like any stored conversation data deleted, please contact us using the information below.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Facebook Page, "Israt's World".
